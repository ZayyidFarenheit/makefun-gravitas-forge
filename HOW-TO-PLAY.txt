GRAVITAS FORGE — HOW TO PLAY
============================

Defend the living Forge-Star at the heart of a miniature solar system.
Survive all 7 waves and destroy the dreadnought THE VOID MAW to win.
If the Forge-Star's Core Integrity hits zero, the Forge falls.

CONTROLS
  Mouse ......... aim (watch the dotted gravity arc — shots bend around moons)
  Click ......... fire the star railgun
  1 ............. build Railgun Turret (40 plasma)
  2 ............. build Plasma Flak (60 plasma, 10 ore)
  3 ............. build Gravity Repulsor (50 plasma, 20 ore)
  4 ............. build Dyson Collector (30 plasma, +4 MW/s)
  Q ............. boost decaying orbits back out (10 plasma)
  X ............. Nova Burst (after unlocking it in the tech tree)
  Click a tech name in the TECH TREE strip to buy it (8 techs, 2 superweapons)
  P ............. pause
  M ............. mute
  Mouse wheel ... zoom the tactical camera
  Enter / R ..... start / restart

TIPS
  - Shield-Cruisers take 75% less damage from the front. Bend your shots
    around the moons (Vey and Ith) to hit their armored flanks.
  - Shoot comets to harvest +15 Solar Plasma each.
  - Structures decay inward over time — press Q before one burns up.
  - Gravity Leeches drain your energy flow; kill them first.

TO RUN THE FULL GAME (from the repo):
  node serve.ts        then open http://localhost:8080
